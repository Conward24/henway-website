Get paid · Setup

Take payments in about 10 minutes.

Stripe pays you. Henway keeps 5% of each payment, and Stripe's card fee comes out of each one.

You need:

An emailYour legal nameLast 4 of your SSNA bank account

Every screen you'll see.

  1. Open Your apps and tap Set up payments.In Henway. The Before you start card says what Stripe will ask for and about how long it takes.
  2. Sign in or make a Stripe account.On Stripe. Use the email you want payout notices sent to.
  3. Pick your business type.Most coaches and hosts pick Individual. No company needed.
  4. Add your details.Legal name, birth date and the last 4 of your SSN. Stripe checks who you are, not Henway.
  5. Website: leave it blank.Henway already told Stripe what you sell, so that part is filled in.
  6. Add the bank for payouts.Payments land there on Stripe's payout schedule.
  7. You're back. Payments are on.In Henway. Payouts and refunds live in your Stripe.
