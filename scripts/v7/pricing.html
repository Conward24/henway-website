Can I change my free app?

Yes. Say what you want different and the app updates. A free app gets 10 changes in all. Keep it for $9 and it gets 30 changes a month.

What happens after 6 months?

If an app goes 6 months with nobody opening it or saving anything, its saved data is cleared. The app itself stays. Apps you keep for $9, and apps on a paid plan, are kept as they are.

Can I get a refund?

Keep an app, $9: if your app doesn't do what you asked and Henway can't fix it, email support@henwayai.com for the $9 back. A refunded app goes back to the free limits.

Build it with Michael, $150: full refund if you cancel at least 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours, you can move it once.

Payments and copies sold through your app: you set your own refund policy. When you refund one, Henway's fee on it goes back too.

What's an AI answer?

Some apps read a photo, suggest something, or talk with you like a tutor. Each reply is one answer, so a chat uses one per message. Free apps get 20 a day. Kept apps get 200.

No. You pay once for each app you keep.
