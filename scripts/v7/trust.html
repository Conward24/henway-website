What goes where.

AI: only what the answer needs.

The photo or PDF, or your question and the bit of data it needs. It goes to Henway and to Anthropic, and comes back as text. Henway doesn't store it.

Training: not used to train AI.

Your content isn't used to train models, ours or anyone's. Anthropic's commercial terms say the same.

Shared lists: stored so your group sees one list.

Turn on a shared list and it lives on Henway's server. Each person agrees before they add to it. Everything else stays on your device.

Reminders: only to people who say yes.

A reminder goes only to someone who confirms it from their own inbox. We keep that email until they stop it, in one tap.

Payments: handled by Stripe.

Card details go straight to Stripe. Henway sees the amount and what it was for, not the card or who paid.

Your data: yours to delete.

Delete an app and its data goes with it. If an app goes 6 months with nobody opening it or saving anything, its saved data is cleared. The app itself stays.

Apps you keep for $9, and apps on a paid plan, are kept as they are. Ask us to erase everything and we do.

Running it for clients? You stay the one they trust.

On Consultant and Agency plans the work runs under your brand. Your client signs nothing of ours.
