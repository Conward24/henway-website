How it runs

What it looks like in the room.

  1. Before. Two minutes, once. Your logo, your colours, your domain on Agency. From then on every session is under your studio’s name.
  2. In the room. Their team describes a normal week out loud. Nobody types, and nobody has to arrive with an idea.
  3. The part that is yours. It reads the problem back and they argue with the wording. That argument is the session, and you are the one running it.
  4. Before anything is built. It says out loud what it can build, what their phones already do, and what needs a real engineering team. You are never the one discovering a limit in front of the client.
  5. Then it builds, while they watch. The tool’s part is about seven minutes. The rest of the room is yours.
  6. They leave with a working app at its own link, and the brief. When they get stuck in another tool a month later, the app tells them to ask you.
