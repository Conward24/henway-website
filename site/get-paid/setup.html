Get paid · Setup

Take payments in about 5 minutes.

Stripe pays you. Henway keeps 5% of each payment, and Stripe's card fee comes out of each one. Here's every screen you'll see.

You need: an email, your legal name, the last 4 of your SSN, and a bank account.

  1. Step 1 · In Henway

    Open Your apps and tap Set up payments.

    Read the Before you start card: what you need, about 5 minutes, and the 5% fee.

  2. Step 2 · Stripe

    Sign in or make a Stripe account.

    Use the email you want payout notices sent to.

  3. Step 3 · Stripe

    Pick your business type.

    Most coaches and hosts pick Individual. No company needed.

  4. Step 4 · Stripe

    Add your details.

    Your legal name, birth date and the last 4 of your SSN. Stripe checks who you are, not Henway.

  5. Step 5 · Stripe

    Confirm your website.

    It's already filled in with your app's link.

  6. Step 6 · Stripe

    Add the bank for payouts.

    Payments land here on Stripe's payout schedule.

  7. Step 7 · In Henway

    You're back. Payments are on.

    Now add a Pay button with Change this app: say "Let clients pay $40 for January."

Add a Pay button with Change this app.

Say what it's for and how much. The button appears.
