PromptFlow

I keep filming myself for these launch videos and I forget what I was going to say halfway through. I want the words scrolling on my phone while I record, big enough to read from arm’s length, and I want to film from the same phone so I’m not looking away from the lens. Let me save a few scripts and pick one. That’s it.

Said out loud, from memory. Not word for word.

Sermon Circle

So, I’m listening to Sarah Jakes Roberts this morning and this message just was dropped on my heart. We’re going to demonstrate an app that allows for virtual Bible study, similar to like a book club. People bring different books, they reflect on a book each week. I’m thinking something with a quicker turnaround time. So I’m thinking we do different sermons. Everybody brings their favorite sermon each week. And then maybe it’s a lottery pool. We can leave comments and vote. We can share the links to the sermons and keep copy on them. I also wanted it to be a way to connect friends and family. Maybe there’s even the time to just sit down and kind of watch as a group and reflect. Coming together, worshiping, reflecting, that is the message, right? That is the goal.

From the voice memo, lightly trimmed. Henway told me first that a live group watch needs a real backend, which it doesn’t have. Everything else, it built.
