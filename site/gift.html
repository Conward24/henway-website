Two apps. My gift to you.

I built both of these with Henway the week of my birthday, by talking, not typing. Open them on your phone and keep them. Below each one is what it does, who it's for, and what I actually said to get it.

PromptFlow. A teleprompter that films from the same screen.

What it does

Write or paste a script, set the size and speed, and it scrolls while you read near the lens. Turn on the front camera right there and record the take on the same screen. Your scripts save on your phone, and you can keep a copy as a file.

Who it's for

Anyone who records themselves and loses the thread. I used it for every video in this launch.

What I said to Henway

I keep filming myself for these launch videos and I forget what I was going to say halfway through. I want the words scrolling on my phone while I record, big enough to read from arm's length, and I want to film from the same phone so I'm not looking away from the lens. Let me save a few scripts and pick one. That's it.

Said out loud, from memory. Not word for word.

Sermon Circle. A sermon-sharing circle for friends and family.

What it does

Everyone brings the sermon that got them that week: a title, a link to watch or listen, a line on why. The circle leaves reflections and amens, a lottery picks the week's sermon, and one tap puts the meeting on your calendar. It keeps its data on your device and can hand a snapshot of the week to someone else by link.

Who it's for

A group that wants to worship and reflect together between Sundays, wherever they are.

What I said to Henway

So, I'm listening to Sarah Jakes Roberts this morning and this message just was dropped on my heart. We're going to demonstrate an app that allows for virtual Bible study, similar to like a book club. People bring different books, they reflect on a book each week. I'm thinking something with a quicker turnaround time. So I'm thinking we do different sermons. Everybody brings their favorite sermon each week. And then maybe it's a lottery pool. We can leave comments and vote. We can share the links to the sermons and keep copy on them. I also wanted it to be a way to connect friends and family. Maybe there's even the time to just sit down and kind of watch as a group and reflect. Coming together, worshiping, reflecting, that is the message, right? That is the goal.

From the voice memo, lightly trimmed. Henway built it from the whole thing, and told me first that a live group watch needs a real backend, which it doesn't have. Everything else, it built.
