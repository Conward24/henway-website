Questions, straight answers

Everything people ask before they start.

What is Henway?

An AI product tool that builds a working first version of your idea in your browser, while you watch. In about seven minutes it finds the problem worth solving and builds the app. It tells you honestly what it can and can't do, and it writes the one-page brief and build message to take it further.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You bring the idea in plain language. Henway handles the part where you'd normally need to know the tools.

Does Henway build the app for me?

Yes. Henway builds a working first version in your browser, tells you honestly what it can and can't do, and hands the working app plus a build message to a full platform when you outgrow it. Your first build is free.

Which build tools can it recommend?

Fourteen, and we own none of them, so the pick is about you: Lovable, Base44, Bolt, v0 by Vercel, Replit Agent, Google AI Studio, Rork, FlutterFlow, Bubble, Glide, Cursor, Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, and IBM Bob.

What if I get stuck in Lovable or Replit?

Ask Henway. Paste what the tool said, or a screenshot, or just a sentence. Henway holds your spec, the honest split, and your working version, so it can tell what went wrong and give you one thing to paste back, why, and what you should see next. Part of every paid plan.

What about compliance, like HIPAA?

Some builds carry rules like HIPAA or SOC 2. Henway flags them and points you to a stack that can meet them. That way you don't ship patient data on a tool that can't sign a BAA.

Is it free?

Yes. Discovery runs are unlimited with no credit card, and your first real build is yours for life. Each result stays live for fifteen minutes; to build again and keep everything you make, paid plans start at $29 a month.

Who's behind it?

Henway is built by Michael Conward, cofounder of MyLÚA Health, an IBM partner company, and a speaker on the IBM Think 2026 stage. Small by design: you get the builder, not a ticket queue.

What happens to my work if Henway goes away?

Nothing. That's by design. Your app is one file you can keep and open offline, whatever grew onto Lovable, Bolt or Glide lives there, and your brief and build message are yours to download. No version of this depends on us being around.
