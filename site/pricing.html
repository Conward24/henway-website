Questions
Can I change my free app?

Yes. Say what you want different and the app updates. Free apps change too.

What happens after 6 months?

If nothing new is saved in a free app for 6 months, what was typed into it is cleared. Keep it for $9, once, and it stays.

What's an AI answer?

Some apps have a button that reads a photo or suggests something. Each tap is one answer. Free apps get 20 a day. Kept apps get 200.

Is $9 a subscription?

No. You pay once for each app you keep.
