The voice memo, and the app it became.

I listened to Sarah Jakes Roberts this morning, hit record, and talked for two minutes about a sermon-sharing club for friends and family. I didn't type a word. This is what I said, and what Henway built.

The memo

What it heard

"I want to build an app for virtual Bible study, like a sermon-sharing book club with friends and family. My struggle is figuring out how to personalize the experience for different worship styles without losing the real goal: connecting people to God together."Henway's read-back, before it asked me three questions.

What it built

Sermon Circle: a rotation of whose week it is, the sermon links, the twelve-week schedule. It told me first that the group discussion thread is what another tool takes further, then built the rest. It opens on your phone or your laptop and keeps its data on your device. Keep it if it's useful.
