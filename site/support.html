Common questions
Where are my apps?

Sign in at app.henwayai.com/login with the email you used. Your apps are under Apps. Built one without an account? It lives at its own link. If you gave us your email, that link is in the email we sent when it was ready.

How do I log in?

Use Google, or type your email and tap the button to get a sign-in link. No password needed. If the link doesn't arrive, check spam, then email us.

How do I change my app?

Open it and tap Change this app. Say what you want different. You see a preview first, and Undo puts it back.

What does $9 get me?

That app is kept as it is, even if it sits unused, with 200 AI answers a day instead of 20, and help from Henway with it. You pay once. There's no subscription.

Why did an AI button stop working?

Each app gets a set number of AI answers a day, shared by everyone who uses it. A photo counts as 2. It resets the next day, or keep the app for $9 to get more.

What happens after 6 months?

If an app goes 6 months with nothing new saved, its saved data is cleared. The app itself stays. Apps you keep for $9, and apps on a paid plan, are kept as they are.

How do I delete my data?

Delete an app from your Apps list and its data goes with it. To erase everything, use Delete my data or email us.

Refunds

Refund policy

  • Keep an app, $9: if your app doesn't do what you asked and we can't fix it, email support@henwayai.com and we refund the $9. A refunded app goes back to the free limits.
  • Build it with Michael, $150: full refund if you cancel at least 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours, you can move it once.
  • Payments and copies sold through your app: you set your own refund policy. When you refund one, Henway's fee on it goes back too.
