Trust

You choose what leaves your phone.

The worry about AI tools is usually about what you have to hand over. With Henway you hand over as little as the job needs, and an app tells you at the moment it sends anything.

What Henway asks for.

Discovery is a person describing their own work in plain words. Henway doesn't ask for a brand book, a deal file, a contract or a client deck.

What it asks for

Sentences first

Tap the problem that sounds like your week, answer a few short questions, say what the thing should be. For most apps, that's the whole input.

Screenshots, files, links

Read, then discarded

You can bring a screenshot, a text file or a link to a public page. A screenshot is read once for its words, then the picture is dropped. A file is read on your phone. A link, we read the page once. You see what was read and can edit it before it joins your conversation. We keep that text, not the picture, file or page, and the screen tells you to check for keys first.

Where the data lives

What you type stays on your device, unless you share it.

By default, an app Henway builds keeps what you put into it in your own browser. If you turn on a shared list, that list is stored on Henway's servers so everyone with the link sees the same thing, and each person agrees before they add to it. If you set a reminder, we keep your email address until you stop it. Download any app as a single file and it runs offline.

When an app asks AI.

Some apps have a button that reads a photo, like a workout sheet or a recipe card, or suggests something from your own log. It tells you before it sends anything.

At the tap

It asks before it sends

The first time, the app says what goes and where: "This photo goes to Henway's AI to read the words, once. It isn't kept." You can type it in instead.

What goes

Only what that answer needs

The photo or PDF, or your question and the part of your data it needs. It goes to Henway's server and to Anthropic to get the answer, and comes back to your app as text.

What stays

Henway keeps none of it

We don't store or log what you send. We count answers, to keep daily limits and costs in check. Anthropic handles it under its commercial terms. The rest of the app's data stays where it was: on your device, or in your shared list.

What we do, and do not do, with what you type.

Training

Not used for training

We don't use your content to train AI models, and we don't provide it to our AI providers for training. Anthropic, whose API runs Henway, states it doesn't train on data sent through its commercial API.

Isolation

One session doesn't touch another

We don't expose or sell your idea, brief or session content, and one person's idea doesn't shape another person's output.

Retention

Cleared when nobody uses it

Discovery transcripts are scrubbed 180 days after last activity. A built app that goes 180 days with nothing saved and no changes keeps working at its link, but what was typed into it is cleared, along with its reminders. An app kept for $9 is skipped. Ask for erasure any time and we run it, kept apps included.

If you run this for clients, you stay the one they trust.

On Consultant and Agency plans the work runs under your brand, and on Agency, on your own domain. Your client is in a room with you, under the agreement you already have. They don't sign up for anything of ours.

That is not a branding feature. It is the reason a firm with a strict client agreement can use this at all.
