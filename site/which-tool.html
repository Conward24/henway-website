How do you start?

Henway: saying what's bugging you.
Prompt-to-code builders: writing a prompt for the app.
AI chat: asking a question.
No-code builders: setting up a spreadsheet or tables.

What do you get?

Henway: a working app at its own link.
Prompt-to-code builders: an app and its code, published on their platform.
AI chat: an answer, sometimes a small app inside the chat.
No-code builders: an app on top of your data.

How do changes work?

Henway: say it. Undo any change.
Prompt-to-code builders: prompt again. The code changes.
AI chat: copy, paste, try again.
No-code builders: change settings by hand.

What does it cost?

Henway: first app free, $9 once to keep.
Prompt-to-code builders: free tier, then monthly (Bolt Pro is $25).
AI chat: free tier, then monthly.
No-code builders: free tier, then monthly (Softr Basic is $19).

Pick something else when...

  • You write code and want to own it. A prompt-to-code builder hands you the project.
  • Thousands of strangers will log in. That's a product with its own backend. Start with a builder made for that, or talk to Studio.
  • Your data already lives in a big spreadsheet. A no-code builder sits right on top of it.
  • You just need to think it through. AI chat is great at that. Come back when you want the app.
